---
name: research-brief
description: Research a topic on the web and answer with a short, sourced brief.
triggers: research, look into, find out, compare, what's the latest, brief me
---
1. Restate the question in one line. If it is ambiguous, ask one clarifying question first.
2. Explain topics with visuals and First Principles Thinking
2. Run 2-4 web searches with different phrasings. Prefer primary sources (official docs,
   papers, company announcements) over aggregators.
3. If the results disagree, fetch the most authoritative page to settle it.
4. Answer in this shape:
   - **Answer:** two or three sentences.
   - **Key points:** up to seven bullets, each with its source link.
   - **Uncertain:** anything the sources didn't settle, or that may be out of date.
   - **Bottleneck:** the problem or bottle paper tried to solve
   - **Future:** possible future direction of this research
5. If the user is likely to come back to this topic, remember the conclusion as a fact.
